🧪 样品管理

样品库管理 + 委托样品信息 (CRM关联)

1. 样品库管理

API：xiziyi-lims-web/src/api/sample/sampleLibraryInfo.js

前端页面：views/sample/sampleLibraryInfo/index.vue

GET/measure/sampleLibraryInfo/list样品列表
GET/measure/sampleLibraryInfo/{id}样品详情
POST/measure/sampleLibraryInfo新增样品
POST/measure/sampleLibraryInfo/importData导入样品

核心数据表：ms_sample_info

-- 样品信息查询 SELECT si.*, et.entrust_no, cc.customer_name FROM ms_sample_info si LEFT JOIN ms_entrust_task et ON si.entrust_id = et.id LEFT JOIN crm_customer cc ON si.customer_id = cc.id WHERE si.del_flag = '0' AND si.entrust_id = #{entrustId} AND si.sample_name LIKE CONCAT('%',#{sampleName},'%') ORDER BY si.create_time DESC
